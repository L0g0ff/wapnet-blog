I have an HP 6560b laptop with windows 8 installed. But HP driver support for Windows 8 is very very bad... I don't go too deep in to de details but the network driver, video driver, auto-update software (HPSoftpaq) and hardware support and sucks big time :X

I bought a bluetooth speaker but the HP wireless assistant isn't available for windows 8 so you can't enable bluetooth. What you must do to get this working is reboot your laptop, press F10, and reset your BIOS to default.

When you boot Windows you will see an bluetooth icon (out-of-the-box windows drivers are working fine :) )
