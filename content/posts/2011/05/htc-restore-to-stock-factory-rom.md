If you must sent your phone back to the factory because of your hardware warranty, make sure your phone is running a stock rom. I will tell you how to restore your phone back to stock.

After this tutorial your phone is completly unrooted, s-on and with a HTC stock radio :)

- Download a stock rom @ [http://shipped-roms.com/](http://shipped-roms.com/)
- Download and install HTC sync @ [http://www.htc.com/www/support.aspx](http://www.htc.com/www/support.aspx)
- Be sure your phone is running a HTC Sense rom (e.g. cyanogenmod cannot revert back to stock)

Ok, lets started

1. Make a backup of all your files
1. Connect your phone to your pc
1. Pull down the android menu and switch from charging to HTC Sync (on the phone)
1. With HTC Sync wizard (PC) give your phone some name (if you run HTC Sync for the first time)
1. Now run the signed exe file your downloaded @ shipped-rom
1. Follow the wizard to flash the rom

Now your phone is back to stock. Enjoy your warranty :D
