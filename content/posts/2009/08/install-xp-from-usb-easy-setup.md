**What you need**

\- [PeToUSB](http://gocoding.com/page.php?al=petousb)
\- Copy of [Windows XP](http://netbooks.modaco.com/content/msi/261632/installing-xp-via-a-usb-stick/#)
\- 1GB USB flash

1: Put your [Windows](http://netbooks.modaco.com/content/msi/261632/installing-xp-via-a-usb-stick/#) disc in your drive.
2: Insert your USB flash.
3: Load PeToUSB
4: Configure it with your sources and destination like this

![](http://content.modaco.net/pe2usb.jpg)

5: Click start
6: Once it's finished remove the USB flash and insert it into your EEE PC. _Set the BIOS to boot from the USB flash._
_7:_ Watch it install!

Ps. @ Windows7 you must manually format the stick to FAT32 and run the software as aministrator

Source: [http://netbooks.modaco.com/content/msi/261632/installing-xp-via-a-usb-stick/](http://netbooks.modaco.com/content/msi/261632/installing-xp-via-a-usb-stick/)
