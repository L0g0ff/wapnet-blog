It's can be hard to find a decent spotify playlist. I thought it must be possible to create a playlist from all of the yesterday tracks of my favourite dutch radio station 3FM. I will describe the steps you must take to create the playlist.

The software you need is [notepad++](http://notepad-plus-plus.org/download). The editing in this article can be done with other editors also but notpad++ has a macro recorder to repeat some actions really quickly.

First you need to find a website which have a full playlist from the radio station. I found one here [http://nummerweten.nl/playlist/radio\_3\_fm\_playlist](http://nummerweten.nl/playlist/radio_3_fm_playlist)

Then you need to copy the list to you clipboard.

[![copy-playlist](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/copy-playlist.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/copy-playlist.png)

Past the copied date in to notepad++

[![past-notepad](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/past-notepad.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/past-notepad.png)

Start the macro recorder an cut of all the garbage of the first line and hop to the second line. TIP: Use CRTL+SHIFT for the selections

[![start record](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/start-record.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/start-record.png)[![clean first line](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/clean-first-line.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/clean-first-line.png)[![stop recoriding](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/stop-recoriding.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/stop-recoriding.png)

Replay the macro

[![notepad replay macro](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/notepad-replay-macro.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/notepad-replay-macro.png)[![run macro multiple tmes](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/run-macro-multiple-tmes.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/run-macro-multiple-tmes.png)

Now you have a clean list
[![clean list](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/clean-list.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/clean-list.png)

Go to [www.ivyishere.org](http://www.ivyishere.org/) \> import your playlist > click copy & past > past the new list in, select "Only include original performances", select your country for better matching and press the upload button.

[![ivy import](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/ivy-import.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/ivy-import.png)

Now let ivy to their magic

[![ivy magic](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/ivy-magic.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/ivy-magic.png)

Click on the image to copy the new playlist to your clipboard

[![ivy copy clipboard](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/ivy-copy-clipboard.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/ivy-copy-clipboard.png)

Create a new spotify playlist and import the tracks with CTRL+V

[![spotify past list](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/spotify-past-list.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/spotify-past-list.png)

Happy listening :)
