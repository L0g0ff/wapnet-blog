A very great freeware tool to real-time monitor your server or workstation is eventsentry @ [http://www.eventsentry.com/](http://www.eventsentry.com/)

This tool wil sent all windows event viewer errors and warnings to your desktop and/or email. You can simply add filters when you don't want to see everything.
