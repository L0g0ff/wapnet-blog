Hi,

Here you can find the starfall homebrew application. With this software you can skip the  required Wii update's in games.

[Download](/wp-content/uploads/2009/12/5.-Starfall.zip)

1. Extract the app in SD\\APPS\
1. Start HBC
1. Start the starfall
1. Press multiple times on B+1 (NOT B+2)
1. Disable the update check
1. Have fun!

When you have 4.0, 4.1 or 4.2 you can use [startpatch](http://wiibrew.org/wiki/StartPatch#Download) if you use 3.4 or 3.6 you must upgrade or downgrade your Wii Firmware.
