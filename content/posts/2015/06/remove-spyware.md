First I had a problem with delta-homes.com spyware. When I remove that software a crappy mystartsearch.com browser hijacker came by... ARGGG!!!!

So removing the software is quite easy when you follow this staps

1. Download [superantispyware](https://www.superantispyware.com/download.html) (free)
1. Install superantispyware (select free editon)
1. Run a quick scan
1. Clean and reboot
1. Run a full scan
1. Clean and reboot
1. Download and run [ADWCleaner](https://toolslib.net/downloads/viewdownload/1-adwcleaner/)
1. Scan and clean
