This week I have my new toy. A raspberry PI :-)

I will post in this article nice articles you definitely needed when you have a Raspberry also.

Extra hardware you need: **SD Card**, **USB Keyboard**, **HDMI Cable**, **USB Wifi module/Ethernet Connection**, **Phone Charger** with **micro USB** connection
Recommended Linux Distribution: Raspbian "Wheezy" @ [http://www.raspberrypi.org/downloads](http://www.raspberrypi.org/downloads) Installation : [http://elinux.org/RPi\_Easy\_SD\_Card\_Setup](http://elinux.org/RPi_Easy_SD_Card_Setup)
Audio: [http://elinux.org/R-Pi\_Troubleshooting#Sound](http://elinux.org/R-Pi_Troubleshooting#Sound)
Alsa fix: [http://www.raspberrypi.org/phpBB3/viewtopic.php?f=66&t=7107](http://www.raspberrypi.org/phpBB3/viewtopic.php?f=66&t=7107)
Wifi: [http://www.raspberrypi-tutorials.co.uk/set-raspberry-pi-wireless-network/](http://www.raspberrypi-tutorials.co.uk/set-raspberry-pi-wireless-network/) Wifi Tip: to get a nice WPA script type the following: #wpa\_passphrase _ssidname ssidpassword_
Which board do you have: [http://elinux.org/RaspberryPi\_Boards](http://elinux.org/RaspberryPi_Boards)
