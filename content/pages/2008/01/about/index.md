Hi, I'm Thomas. I've been writing on this blog since 2008, mostly about the things that cost me an evening to figure out, so they don't have to cost you one.

**Day Job: Managed Services**

I'm a Managed Services Specialist (Infrastructure) at [Infield ICT](https://infield-ict.nl/), part of [co-one](https://co-one.com/). I mostly work for hospitals, where systems simply can't go down. That means keeping their infrastructure running: Linux and Windows servers, hybrid cloud, networks, CI/CD pipelines and monitoring. Lots of troubleshooting, and making processes a bit more boring in the good way.

**Side Business: Re-Scan Monitor Solutions**

Next to that I run [Re-Scan Monitor Solutions](https://www.re-scan.nl/): PRTG consultancy, managed PRTG hosting and training. The goal is simple: your monitoring should spot a problem before your customers do. I like teaching teams to build their own sensors and dashboards, and lately I'm also looking hard at open source monitoring like Zabbix.

**Open Source and Side Projects**

- [KompassOS](https://kompassos.nl/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/L0g0ff/KompassOS)): an atomic KDE desktop built on Aurora, made for DevOps engineers, system administrators and network engineers. A ready-to-use workstation without hours of setup, with extra love for Surface devices.
- Monitoring bits and pieces, like a [PRTG dashboard](https://github.com/L0g0ff/prtg-dashboard) in plain HTML, JavaScript and CSS.
- A homelab where Docker, cameras, notes, a kanban board and a self-hosted AI agent all live together. Most of my recent posts started there.
- Hardware tinkering: pixel clocks, RFID on the Flipper Zero, and anything else with an API.

More on [GitHub](https://github.com/L0g0ff).

**Off the Clock**

My wife, our three kids and our dog, who makes sure I walk my kilometres every day. Beyond that: security and privacy (de-Googled phones and Linux wherever I can), gaming with the kids, movies and series, drum 'n bass, and tech, tech, t3ch. :D

**What I Work With**

Linux (Fedora Atomic, NixOS, Ubuntu), Docker and Podman, Nutanix and Azure, GitLab and GitHub pipelines, Ansible, PowerShell and Python, and of course PRTG and Zabbix. I started out on Windows 3.11 and NT 4.0, so I've seen a few generations of "this will make IT simple". More on my [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thomas-faddegon/).
