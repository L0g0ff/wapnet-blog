- Click edit > Mass Edit > Monitor Properties

[![ipmonitor - monitor properties](/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/ipmonitor-monitor-properties.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/ipmonitor-monitor-properties.png)

- "Add" Monitors where "property" "Type" (Object Type) Matches regular  expression: "drive"
- Replace "Minimum Threshold" by "Overwrite".  Replace with: "10"

[![ipmonitor - mass edit](/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/ipmonitor-mass-edit.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/ipmonitor-mass-edit.png)

- Click "Preview" to ensure the proper values are being changed.
- Click "Next"
