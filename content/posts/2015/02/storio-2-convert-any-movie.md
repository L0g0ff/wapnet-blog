It is possible to convert any movie to play it on a storio 2. The steps you must take:

- [Download SUPER © from my blogpost](/2015/02/super-portable-without-adware/ "SUPER © portable without adware"). Attention: The default [SUPER ©](http://www.videohelp.com/tools/SUPER) is adware and your pc will have some new unwanted browser searches after the install :(
- Configure SUPER © (see screen)

[![SUPER-settings-storio](/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/SUPER-settings-storio.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/SUPER-settings-storio.png)

- Convert / encode the movie
- Upload it to your storio and have fun :)

TIP: Set the Frame/Sec on 10 if you want to reduce the size of the move. 10 FPS is low but good enough for the Storio
