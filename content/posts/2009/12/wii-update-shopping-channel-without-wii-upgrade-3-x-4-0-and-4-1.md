Nintendo give the Opera Wii browser for free now. But when you start the Shopping channel on a softmodded Wii you must update your Wii software first. Because you have a soft/hard mod you don't want that.

1. First download this packages ([DOPIOS](/wp-content/uploads/2009/12/Dop_ios_by_marc.zip) and [WiiSCU](/wp-content/uploads/2009/12/WiiSCU.zip))
1. Extract the both apps to the SD\\APPS\ folder
1. Start DOPIOS from the HBC and update/download IOS61 (the one for the shopping channel)
1. Start WiiSCU from the HBC and update the shopping channel
1. Now you can start the shopping shopping channel from the main screen.

Other info about both apps you can find on [Wiibrew.org](http://Wiibrew.org). Happy hacking!!!
